Our Mission

Love God, Love Others, and Make Disciples

God has a mission and he invites us to join him in what he is doing. Our lives are to be dedicated to loving God in all that we do, loving others every opportunity we get, and making as many disciples as possible.

Commit to Community

When we become Christians, God gives us a family. We are to be present when the church gathers to encourage and uplift one another, to celebrate the joys of life together and support one another in times of sorrow.

Invest and Invite

We make disciples by investing in others and inviting them to worship and serve alongside us. We are to use dinner tables just as Jesus did to get to know people and let them know we care.

Redeem Our County

If there are people in need, we are to help them. If there are people who are lost, we are to tell them about Jesus. Our mission is to leave our mark on the places where we live so that others will come to know God and glorify him.

La Grange Church of Christ

La Grange Church of Christ is a group of Christians who strive to follow God and serve Him to the best of our ability.
